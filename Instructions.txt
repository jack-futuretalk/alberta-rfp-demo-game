Group activity: Spot the Claim
This is Jack's Claim Checker, cited as the culminating group activity.
Text:

You'll see food and wellness ads one at a time. For each one, decide:
Is this claim backed by evidence?
Is it not backed by evidence?
Or does this ad not actually make a claim at all?

That third option matters. Most of the ads we looked at in Topics 1 and 2 never said anything you could check — and that was the whole point of them. If you find yourself reaching for true or false every time, you've already been caught by a soft sell.

After each round you'll see what the ad literally claims, what the evidence actually says, which of the four techniques it used, and where you could go to verify it yourself.

Run it as a class: put it on the board and vote as a group before revealing. The disagreements are the lesson.
